E-Way Bill › E-way bills from Excel

Many e-way bills at once from Excel

The portal's bulk generation takes one JSON file with many e-way bills. Fill our Excel template, one row per item, and the maker turns it into that file, checking each e-way bill against the portal's own rules first.

Step by step

  1. Choose your business in step 1 of the Excel section (from your saved businesses; add one in My data if needed).
  2. Download the Excel template. The How to fill sheet explains every column.
  3. One row per item. Rows with the same Document No make one e-way bill; the buyer and transport details are read from its first row.
  4. Choose the filled file (.xlsx or CSV UTF-8). Fix any rows the list shows, save, and choose it again.
  5. Download the e-way bill file and upload it at ewaybillgst.gov.in › e-Waybill › Generate Bulk › Choose file › Generate.
  6. The portal lists the e-way bill numbers. Press Export to Excel there and choose that file in step 3 here: the numbers go onto your saved invoices and into the Part B table.
  7. No vehicle yet? Fill the Part B table later and upload the vehicle file under e-Waybill › Update Vehicle Bulk.

Which document for which reason

The portal accepts only these pairs for goods going out (NIC bulk validations):

Sub Supply Type (reason)Document TypeGoes to
SupplyINV tax invoice, BIL bill of supplyBuyer (GSTIN or unregistered)
ExportINV, BILUnregistered (URP), outside India
Job WorkCHL delivery challanJob worker
SKD/CKD/LotsINV, BIL, CHLBuyer
For Own Use, Exhibition or Fairs, Line SalesCHLYourself (your own GSTIN)
Recipient Not KnownCHL, OTHYourself
Others (say what)CHL, OTHYourself or another party

With a bill of supply, a delivery challan or "others", the tax rate must be 0 on every item. For "own use", exhibitions, line sales and "recipient not known" the maker puts your own GSTIN as the receiver for you.

The transport columns

ColumnWhat to type
Distance kmUp to 4,000. 0 lets the portal work it out from the PIN codes.
Transport ModeRoad, Rail, Air or Ship. Empty (with no vehicle) = Part A only.
Vehicle No, Vehicle TypeLike PB10AB1234; R regular, O over-dimensional cargo
Transporter ID, NameTransporter's GSTIN or 15-character TRANSIN. By road, give this or the vehicle.
Transport Doc No, DateLR, RR or airway bill. Needed for rail and air.
Ship To Address, City, PIN, State CodeOnly when the goods go to another place than the buyer's address; for an export, the port in India.

A worked example

A Ludhiana garment unit sends cut fabric to a job worker in Panipat (Haryana) on challan DC/26-27/045, worth ₹38,000. Job work to another state needs an e-way bill at any value. Row: Document No DC/26-27/045, Document Type CHL, Sub Supply Type Job Work, GST % 0, Transport Mode Road, Vehicle No PB10AB1234, Distance 0.

Try a small file first. If the portal rejects a row, it names the field: fix it in Excel and make the file again.
